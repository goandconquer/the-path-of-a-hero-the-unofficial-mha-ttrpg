Stun Techniques cause a Target to lose a certain Action Points depending on its Rank. After upgrading the Technique past Rank D, the Player may add an additional benefit based on the Sensei's approval.

Targets that lose more than 3 Action Points have the opportunity to Roll against the Player's initial Quirk Focus check. If they fail, they lose up to three (3) Action Points on their next Turn. If they succeed, they escape the stun and no longer lose Action Points.
