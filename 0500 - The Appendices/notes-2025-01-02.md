A character with a Transformation Quirk receives one Transformation Technique that does not cost Technique Points to have. All additional Transformation Techniques follow the normal rules related to Technique Point costs.

This initial Transformation Technique has its rank equal to the Control Rank of the character at all times. This equivalency also determines the Limit Point cost associated with this technique.
